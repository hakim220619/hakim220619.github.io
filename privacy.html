Privacy Policy

video upload · Last updated: 17 November 2026

What this app is

video upload is a private tool used only by the owner of a YouTube channel, on the owner's own computer, to upload and schedule the owner's own videos.

Google user data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app requests these Google OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload video files to the owner's channel.
  • youtube: to set the title, description, tags, scheduled publish time and thumbnail of those videos.

The app does not read, collect or analyse any other data from the Google account.

How the data is used and stored

  • The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used only to perform the uploads the owner starts.
  • No data is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The app has no analytics, cookies or tracking.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the computer also removes all stored data.
